Our Philosophy

Since 2005, every Buyer's side transaction representation has involved a commission credit — which has made a lot of Buyers happy over the years. Our view of the real estate industry is one of opportunity to provide a win-win, so Buyer and Agent both feel satisfied at Close of Escrow.

In today's world, with the internet and, more specifically, information literally at your fingertips in a split second, Buyers like you are extremely tech-savvy and do a bulk of independent real estate research, homework, and internet searches upfront. Many Buyers have put in the time, the effort, the research, and the analysis to find the perfect area or neighborhood they wish to live in well before ever talking to a Real Estate Agent.
