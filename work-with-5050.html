The Steps to Purchase a New Home - 5050 Cliff Note Version

Five Steps, Zero Surprises

01
Decide to Purchase a New Build
01

You Decide to Purchase a New Build Home!

You've searched homes online. You've crunched the numbers. You have made the decision to purchase a new home! Purchasing a new home is truly exciting. New construction homes are energy efficient and are pre-wired for all of today's smart technology. Everything in the home is shiny and brand new — purchasing a brand new home is an exciting new chapter.

02
Contact 5050 First!
02

Make Contact With 5050 Real Estate Before You Register With the New Home Builder

New builders offer commission to a real estate agent who brings a buyer to one of their communities for a home purchase. The most important part of the process is that the real estate agent must register the buyer before the buyer tours a model home on their own and registers themselves with the home builder.

If you, the buyer, were to register yourself first without the representation of a real estate agent, the builder will offer no commission on the home sale. Even if a real estate agent were to attempt to get involved in the transaction after the fact, the builder will still deny commission on the sale.

Therefore it is critical, important, and essential to connect with 5050 Real Estate first!

03
Let 5050 Register You With the Builder
03

5050 Real Estate Will Register You With the New Home Builder

Timing of registration is extremely important. A real estate agent must register you first with the home builder. If you, the buyer, register yourself online at a builder's website or in-person at a model home without the representation of a real estate agent, the new home builder will not offer a commission on the sale.

If you call 5050 Real Estate first, we will register you with the builder, either in-person at the model home or online (every builder has their own rules for registration). With a successful close of escrow, 5050 Real Estate will credit you, the buyer, 50% of the net commission at close of escrow — potentially lowering the overall cost of your new home by thousands of dollars.

04

04

Tour Model Homes and Decide on Your New Home and Lot

This honestly is the best part of the process. Model homes are absolutely beautiful! You get to see all the wonderful homes with all the amazing amenities. When walking and touring a model home, you can actually picture yourself and/or your family living in the home. 5050 Real Estate can help you decide the best neighborhood for purchase and the best lot to select for your new home build.

05

05

Close Escrow on Your Beautiful New Home

The date has finally arrived! Your new home is fully constructed and ready for move-in. It's time to sign all documents from the title company to officially make the new home yours. When you are reviewing your closing documentation, you'll happily see the commission credit from 5050 Real Estate which lowered the overall cost of your home.
